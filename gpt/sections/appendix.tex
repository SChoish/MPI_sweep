\section{Proofs}
\label{app:proofs}

\subsection{Behavior-anchor decomposition}
For $b(s)=\E[a\mid s]$,
\begin{align*}
\E[\|\mu(s)-a\|_2^2\mid s]
&=\E[\|\mu(s)-b(s)+b(s)-a\|_2^2\mid s]\\
&=\|\mu(s)-b(s)\|_2^2\\
&\quad+\E[\|a-b(s)\|_2^2\mid s],
\end{align*}
because the cross term vanishes conditionally. Averaging over states and dividing by $d$ proves Eq.~\eqref{eq:biasvariance}. For Dirac policies, the only coupling places the mass at $(\mu(s),\nu(s))$, hence $W_2^2(\delta_{\mu(s)},\delta_{\nu(s)})=\|\mu(s)-\nu(s)\|_2^2$.

\subsection{Proof of Proposition~\ref{prop:conditional_descent}}
The assumed comparator inequality is
\begin{align*}
-&\frac{2h}{C_k}\E_s\widehat Q_1(s,\mu_k(s))
+\mathsf d_\rho^2(\mu_k,\mu_{k-1})\\
&\leq-\frac{2h}{C_k}\E_s\widehat Q_1(s,\mu_{k-1}(s))+\varepsilon_k.
\end{align*}
Rearranging gives Eq.~\eqref{eq:conditional_descent}. Writing $\widehat Q_1=Q^\dagger+e$ and collecting the $e$ terms gives Eq.~\eqref{eq:error_transfer}.

\subsection{Proof of Proposition~\ref{prop:target_perturbation}}
Let $F_{s'}(a)=\min_jQ_j^-(s',a)$. Since the minimum of functions sharing Lipschitz constant $L(s')$ is also $L(s')$-Lipschitz,
\begin{equation*}
 |F_{s'}(a)-F_{s'}(b)|\leq L(s')\|a-b\|_2.
\end{equation*}
Therefore,
\begin{equation*}
 |y_{\mu_1}-y_{\mu_0}|\leq\gamma L(s')\|\mu_1(s')-\mu_0(s')\|_2.
\end{equation*}
Squaring, averaging over transitions, and taking a square root proves Eq.~\eqref{eq:target_bound}.

\subsection{Local explicit--proximal expansion}
The statewise first-order condition of the exact proximal objective gives
\begin{equation*}
 \Delta a=\beta\nabla_a\widehat Q_1(a_0+\Delta a),\qquad \beta=dh/C.
\end{equation*}
Taylor expansion yields
\begin{equation*}
 (I-\beta H)\Delta a=\beta g+O(\beta\|\Delta a\|^2).
\end{equation*}
For sufficiently small $\beta$ with $\beta\|H\|<1$, Neumann expansion gives Eq.~\eqref{eq:local_expansion}. This calculation concerns an exact action-space solution under a fixed smooth critic. It does not describe one Adam step through a ReLU network at large $T$.

\section{Artifact and Claim Ledger}
\label{app:ledger}

\paragraph{Directly recomputable from committed matrices.}
The central sweep curves, aggregate table, $K=3$ versus $K=4$ cell comparison, threshold sensitivity, per-environment high-budget table, and one-hop replication are generated from CSV snapshots in \texttt{gpt/data} and the repository's \texttt{sweep\_results} directory. The figure script also writes the derived cell-transition counts to \texttt{data/derived\_metrics.json}.

\paragraph{Present only as aggregate claims in the prior bundle.}
The target-exposure audit, common-critic gains, simulator-reference screening, shadow-critic intervention, fixed-reference control, target-lag control, and frozen-critic local audit are described in the pre-existing manuscript sources. Their raw transition-level outputs, checkpoints, and analysis scripts are not present in the repository snapshot reviewed here. They are therefore not used as headline numerical evidence in this revision.

\paragraph{Incomplete replications at the snapshot.}
The additional proximal $K=2$ seeds contain 232 of 252 planned cells, while proximal $K=3$ seeds 2--3 were not yet present. These incomplete matrices are excluded from all tables. The status is frozen in \texttt{data/replication\_status.csv}.

\paragraph{Source/build drift.}
The uploaded PDF predates several source-level additions, including the complete linearized $K=3$ result and targeted controls. This bundle compiles the PDF from its own exact sources and includes a source manifest. The official AISTATS 2027 style was not available in the reviewed package; the included style is explicitly provisional.

\section{Recommended Controlled Rerun}
\label{app:rerun}

The highest-value rerun should preserve actor~1 and critic initialization across $K$. One safe key allocation is
\begin{equation*}
 k_{\rm actor1},k_{\rm critic},k_{\rm aux}
 =\operatorname{split}(k_{\rm seed},3),
\end{equation*}
then initialize auxiliary actors from $\operatorname{split}(k_{\rm aux},K-1)$. The comparison should additionally include: (i) auxiliary actors initialized as copies of their references versus independent networks, (ii) a complete-grid compute-matched control, (iii) a target-exposure-matched one-hop control, and (iv) logging of the premise in Proposition~\ref{prop:conditional_descent}. At minimum, log pre/post actor objective, learned-critic gain, step displacement, target-policy displacement, and the exact critic/actor initialization hashes.
