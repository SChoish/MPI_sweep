\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Offline policy regularization.}
Behavior-constrained offline RL limits policy shift through action support, divergence penalties, or behavior cloning \citep{fujimoto2019offpolicy,kumar2019bear,wu2019brac,fujimoto2021minimalist,tarasov2023revisiting}. Advantage-weighted extraction and pessimistic value learning provide complementary mechanisms \citep{nair2020awac,kostrikov2022iql,kumar2020cql,an2021edac}. MPI does not introduce a new critic penalty. It changes the actor architecture so that the branch entering bootstrapping is not the branch ultimately deployed.

\paragraph{Target policies and decoupled extraction.}
One-step offline RL freezes a value estimate before a single constrained improvement, while backup-side constraints restrict the actions used in the Bellman operator \citep{brandfonbrener2021onestep,kumar2019bear,ghasemipour2021emaq}. Decoupled extraction can train a behavior model separately and use value guidance only at inference \citep{lin2026decoupling}. MPI is narrower: it retains end-to-end TD3+BC training but routes the critic through the first member of a multi-actor chain.

\paragraph{Proximal policy optimization and geometry.}
Natural gradients and trust regions are associated with Fisher geometry \citep{amari2000methods,schulman2015trust}. Wasserstein geometry has also been used to formulate policy-gradient flows, natural-gradient methods, and transport-based trust regions \citep{zhang2018policy,moskovitz2020efficient,terpin2022trust,song2023metric}. In offline RL, Wasserstein discrepancies have mainly served as behavior regularizers or distribution-matching objectives \citep{wu2019brac,asadulaev2024rethinking}. Here the deterministic squared anchor is interpreted as a fiberwise Wasserstein movement cost, but the implementation remains a neural proximal-loss update rather than an exact minimizing-movement solver.

\paragraph{Expressive and iterative policy extraction.}
Flow- and diffusion-based actors increase the expressivity of offline policy extraction \citep{park2025flow,wang2023diffusion}. Expressivity does not by itself determine how far a critic should move a policy or which branch should enter bootstrapping. MPI isolates a complementary design dimension: repeated local actor fitting with separate target and deployment roles.
