# BAR-P3 / MCEP-inspired policy-separation control

This compact archive covers 90 matched final-score pairs: nine D4RL locomotion
tasks, five high budgets (`tau` in {4, 7, 10, 14, 20}), and seeds 0--1. The
comparison uses the 1M-update, 10-episode D4RL normalized return from a
contemporaneous BAR-P3 rerun and a separately trained two-actor control.

The control uses a dataset-anchored target actor at `tau/3` and a separate
dataset-anchored deployment actor at `tau`; only the former enters Bellman
backups. It is inspired by MCEP target/evaluation separation, but it is not a
published-MCEP reproduction or a compute-matched re-centering ablation.

`AUDIT.json` is the completeness, shared-configuration, and paired-key
snapshot generated by `scripts/diagnostics/audit_bar_mcep_p3_paired.py`.
`paired_final_scores.csv` is the canonical compact score source, and
`SUMMARY.json` records the estimand, protocol, independently recomputable
aggregates, execution qualification, and interpretation.
`SOURCE_MANIFEST.csv` identifies all 180 raw
config/eval/final-checkpoint artifacts and available logs by relative path,
size, and SHA-256; the large raw artifacts are not committed.

The control minus BAR mean is +1.5552, with a task-resampling 95% interval
[-1.5187, 5.4433]. Its paired median is -0.4187; BAR wins 52 pairs, the
control wins 34, and four tie. After averaging the two seeds, both procedures
collapse in 9/45 task--budget cells. Sequential re-centering is therefore not
a prerequisite for the observed mean and seed-mean collapse outcomes on this
grid. The bundled comparison does not establish superiority, equivalence, or
which simpler-procedure element is responsible.

The contemporaneous BAR-P3 mean is only +0.1387 above the released P3
aggregate on the same cells, but cellwise differences have RMSE 24.03 and range
from -99.50 to +98.63. The archive therefore pairs the control only with the
contemporaneous rerun; matching seed labels do not imply exact trajectory
replication.

Ten Walker2d-expert control cells completed through the CPU recovery chain:
eight resumed at matching logged emergency-checkpoint steps, and two `tau=20`
cells started there from zero. The other 80 are GPU-designated by launch/log
convention. Removing the whole recovery task changes the mean delta to +1.8278,
so recovery is not the source of the aggregate sign, but that deletion cannot
separate hardware from task effects.
